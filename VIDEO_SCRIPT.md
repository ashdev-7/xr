**0:00–0:20 · Hook** (slide 2, degradation figure)
> "An aircraft is flying with GPS jammed. Its only sensor is an IMU, and the jamming keeps getting worse:
> more noise, drifting biases, spikes, lost frames, until the IMU dies and we must dead-reckon five more
> seconds. Integrating the accelerometer twice puts you thousands of metres off. This is PathGuard."

**0:20–0:45 · The problem in our words** (slide 2)
> "We get a raw EuRoC-format IMU log and must output a 6-DoF pose for every row, including dead rows,
> within 8 MB and 20 ms per second of data, in Python. The hard part is physics: an IMU cannot see slow
> motion, because a tiny bias grows into metres within seconds."

**0:45–1:20 · Live run** (terminal)
```bash
python src/main.py sample/imu0/data.csv estimated_trajectory.txt --verbose=1
head -3 estimated_trajectory.txt
python tests/test_contract.py
```
> "One standard-library Python file, nothing to install. The log shows what it found: the platform
> (a rotorcraft, from its rotor vibration), the jamming level read from packet loss, frozen and
> duplicated rows, repaired spikes and rest periods. It runs in about {ms_per_s} ms per second of IMU
> with a {heap} MB heap. One pose per input row, always, as checked by 18 malformed-input tests."

**1:20–1:55 · How it works** (slide 4)
> "First, one streaming pass cleans the data and measures the jamming. Then an error-state Kalman
> smoother estimates attitude from gyro, gravity and rest periods. Position comes from a second smoother
> with a motion prior learned per platform. For drones we add something most people miss: rotor drag
> makes the sideways accelerometer reading proportional to velocity, so the accelerometer becomes a
> velocity sensor. Finally, every input row gets exactly one pose."

**1:55–2:35 · Results** (slide 5, then slide 8)
> "We validated on real EuRoC and TUM-VI IMU data, degraded with the organizers' own script and
> evaluated on flights we never tuned on. On drone flights PathGuard reaches {rotor_ate} metres ATE,
> against {rotor_still} for standing still and {rotor_naive} for naive integration. Every component is
> backed by an ablation, and ideas that did not help were removed and logged."

**2:35–2:50 · Close** (slide 6)
> "Physics-first, measured at every step, and inside every budget. PathGuard. Thank you."
